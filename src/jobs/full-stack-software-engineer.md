  **Compliance Screening**


  This is the most important step. Every profile is checked against the project’s minimum requirements, which are applied strictly:

  Years of experience: counted precisely, sometimes split into general IT experience and experience in a specific domain

  Education level: diploma requirements are often linked to experience thresholds (for example, fewer years required with a Master’s degree)

  Language skills: assessed on the CEFR scale, typically English at B2/C1 level


  **Technical Interview**


  The candidate meets the end client’s project team for a technical interview, which often includes a live technical discussion. In some cases, an internal technical screening takes place first.


  **Pre-Onboarding**


  Before the start date, the candidate provides supporting documents: diplomas, employment references, certificates, and ID.
